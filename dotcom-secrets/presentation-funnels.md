# Presentation funnels: video sales letter, webinar, product launch

At lower prices a demo or a Who, What, Why, How video shows the value in minutes. At higher prices you raise perceived value with a sales presentation, and the Perfect Webinar structure fits any presentation, on stage or online.

A presentation's job: "educate the person on the problem they have, break the false beliefs that are keeping them from moving forward with you, and get them to take action now." Structured correctly, it takes cold traffic all the way along the awareness continuum (Unaware to Most Aware): they see the problem, discount every other solution, become aware of your product and give you money. Depending on the funnel, that happens in minutes, hours, days or weeks.

| Funnel | Presentation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Video sales letter | 20 to 30 minutes | like a book or cart funnel, but the offer needs longer explaining or false beliefs broken before the order form; low ticket up to about $100; also membership sites and continuity |
| Webinar | 60 to 120 minutes, live or automated | $100 to $2,000 |
| Product launch | the same presentation dripped over days or weeks | a new product at $100 to $2,000; builds anticipation and pressure for the release |

## Video sales letter funnel

Pages: **sales page** (headline, video, bullets, Add to Cart) → **bump** → **upsell 1** → **upsell 2** → **thank-you page** (access, next step).

Long-form sales letters online were modeled on mailed direct response ("we didn't change how we sold things; we just changed the media"). The first VSLs showed the letter's text one sentence at a time with a voice-over: visitors couldn't scroll ahead to the price and had to take the message in the designed order, and the Add to Cart button appeared under the video after about 30 minutes. Styles have since varied (hand-drawn animation, the Attractive Character talking to camera), but "the most important part of your video is the presentation"; how you present it matters less than the words.

**Page 1, sales page.** The order form stays off the top, since the presentation must raise perceived value before they see the price. Everything at the top exists to get them to watch, and the curiosity headline's only job is to start the video. His Funnel University page: "WEIRD MARKETING EXPERIMENTS THAT WILL INCREASE YOUR TRAFFIC, CONVERSIONS AND SALES ONLINE..." over a video titled "My Weird Niche Funnel That's Currently Making Me $17,947 Per Day... And How To ETHICALLY Knock It Off In Less Than 10 Minutes!"
- The video script: Star, Story, Solution, or the Perfect Webinar. Either grabs attention, breaks the false beliefs and presents the offer irresistibly.
- A **video spoiler box** below the video pushes them back into watching. It opens "In Today's Free Presentation" and has four blocks: one for each of the three secrets, and "What's The Next Step?" hinting at the call to action. His: Secret #1 Tiny Tweaks Can Make HUGE Changes; Secret #2 Not All People Buy The Same Way; Secret #3 There Are Only 3 Funnels That Actually Matter; What's The Next Step? (a 14-day free trial of Funnel University plus a free Funnel Stacking book). It stays up for the whole presentation.
- A call-to-action section below the spoiler box points to the Order button. Hiding it until the video's pitch versus showing it throughout has given mixed results, so test it with your traffic.

**Page 2, upsells**: the same as book and cart funnels (information products sell the next thing; physical products sell more of the same at a discount).

**Page 3, thank-you page**: an offer wall for physical products; for information products, an invitation into the next funnel.

## Webinar funnel

Pages: **registration** (email, date, time) → **thank-you page** (your ticket, countdown) → **webinar room** (video, slides, Get Started) → **order form**, plus a **replay page**. Bumps, upsells, downsells and a thank-you page can follow the order form.

At Grant Cardone's 10X event (9,000+ people at Mandalay Bay), Brunson was introduced by an emcee (the pre-frame), delivered the 90-minute Perfect Webinar slide by slide, offered a $2,997 course with a free year of ClickFunnels, and caused the biggest table rush he'd seen; sales went from $650,000 to $3.2 million from that one presentation. One talk to 9,000 people beats one-on-one, and through webinar and automated webinar funnels the same presentation is seen more than 30,000 times a month: the stage doesn't scale, and webinar funnels do. In the first year of ClickFunnels he ran at least one live webinar a day, often two or three, and switched to automated only after a year of perfecting it. Rule: run the live webinar funnel as long as you can before automating; live is the most important thing to master.

**Page 1, registration page.** One job: get the registration. A Curiosity-Based Headline, no story, all focus on the hook. His: a strange photo; "Free Training Webinar Reveals... 'My Weird Niche Funnel That's Currently Making Me $17,947 PER DAY! And How To Ethically Knock It Off In Less Than 10 Minutes!'"; "REGISTER MY Seat NOW (Very Limited Seating – Only 200 Seats Per Webinar)"; the date and time; three secrets they'll learn ("How To Ethically Steal Over $1,000,000 Worth Of Funnel Hacks From Your Competitors, For Under $100"; "How To Clone Their PROVEN Funnel In Less Than 10 Minutes!"; "How To Get Their SAME Customers To Buy Your Products (Without Them Knowing You Exist!)"); and a bonus for showing up live (a fully working funnel and membership site, a $5,000 value). The headline raises many questions and answers none. Say when the class starts so they can put it on the calendar. The Register button opens a pop-up for the email, which enters a Soap Opera Sequence that warms them up before the presentation. Run it through webinar software (GoToWebinar or Zoom) connected to the funnel.

**Page 2, thank-you page.** Build the relationship and get them to show up. A Who, What, Why, How video: who I am, what you'll learn, why it matters, how to get it on your calendar. His: "CONGRATS, YOU'RE BOOKED FOR THE WEBINAR!" with three steps before class (write down the date and time; get a free ClickFunnels account; show up live for the $5,000 bonus), the event ticket, and a free *DotCom Secrets* book. Push them into a low-ticket offer (a book or cart funnel) here: it helps them prepare, and it recoups most of the registration ad spend, so the webinar sales are pure profit. This offer must complement the webinar offer and create more need for it; an offer that competes with it cannibalizes the webinar.

**Page 3, webinar room.** In the webinar software: notifications, slides, and the Perfect Webinar delivered. Mastering the presentation is the difference between a flop and millions.

**Page 4, order form.** The call to action at the end sends them here. Reselling on the order form has always hurt his sales; its job is to recap the offer and let them buy. His Funnel Builder Secrets order form ($2,997) lists "Here's What You Get" (12 months of ClickFunnels, a masterclass, the course, Traffic Secrets, Funnel Scripts, total value $11,552). Add bumps, upsells, downsells, offer walls, or a link to a phone funnel on the thank-you page.

**Page 5, replay page.** About 80 percent of registrants won't show up live. Send them to a replay page with the recording and a 48- or 72-hour countdown before it comes down; without that urgency and scarcity, it's hard to get that 80 percent to watch. After the live webinar, email what they missed and how long until the replay is pulled.

### Automated webinar variation

Pages: **registration** → **webinar** (presentation page) → **order form**. No live event or outside software: a prerecorded presentation they register to watch now or at a set time, still promoted with curiosity as an event starting in a few minutes. Registration page: simple, a curiosity headline, an eye-catching image, all focus on Register ("This is a FREE Webclass. There Are No Costs, But Time Is Very Limited"; "Class Begins: TODAY @ 10:30 AM"; or a "FREE 48 Hour Pass"). The presentation page comes right after registering and looks like a replay page ("MAKE SURE YOUR SPEAKERS ARE ON AND CLICK PLAY," a countdown on the 48-hour pass, a Read Transcript button, time stamps). The order form only recaps (his Funnel Scripts order form: $797, with a stack valued at $127,394). Email sequences push them to watch before it expires. Automate only a presentation you have mastered: do it live until then, and use the best-converting live recording as the automated video.

## Product launch funnel

Pages: **"Free Workshop" opt-in** (email, submit) → **video 1** → **video 2** → **video 3** → **video 4** (Add to Cart) → **thank-you page**, with four tabs unlocked one at a time.

Hollywood launches a movie with a trailer, ads with a release date, another trailer, ticket sales, talk-show appearances, opening weekend and word of mouth. Jeff Walker's **sideways sales letter** does the same with a sales letter: the presentation tipped on its side, one part delivered each day to build anticipation and buzz. Sales letter → videos: Secret #1 "The Vehicle" → Video #1 "Wow & How"; Secret #2 "Internal Beliefs" → Video #2 "Transformational Education"; Secret #3 "External Beliefs" → Video #3 "External Forces"; "The Offer" → Video #4 "The Offer."

It works best with warm or hot traffic: a link emailed to your own list or an affiliate or joint-venture partner's list. Teach in the first three videos, sell in the fourth. They register for a free online workshop → Video 1 hooks them to watch Video 2 → a few days later Video 2 arrives and asks for comments, then hooks to the next → Video 3 finishes the presentation and sets up the special offer → Video 4 makes the offer and pushes to the order form.

**Page 1, "Free Workshop" page.** A simple squeeze page whose lead magnet is the training workshop itself (they register for access to the training), with screenshots of the videos at the bottom showing a series that gets a specific result. His: "FREE!!! 'Funnel Workshop' / Free Videos Reveals The #1 Secret To Growing Your Company FAST With Sales Funnels...", with thumbnails for Video #1 Funnel Hacking, #2 Funnel Cloning, #3 My #1 Traffic Hack, #4 Instant Success. Scripts: a Curiosity-Based Headline plus Who, What, Why, How.

**Pages 2–4, video pages.** Thumbnails of the series across the top: released videos in color with a Play button (so they can go back to one they missed), unreleased ones in black and white or with a lock. The video sits below, scripted with the Product Launch script (very close to the Perfect Webinar). Video #1 gives the "wow" of the big idea and shows "how" you and others use it; Video #2 lets them look over your shoulder as you walk through the process; Video #3 breaks the external false beliefs and shows how to get past external roadblocks. Under each video, links to free downloadable lead magnets tied to the teaching, and a comments section that builds buzz, social proof and community. His call to action under the videos: "Step #1 - Get Your FREE 2 Week Trial To Click Funnels NOW!"

**Page 5, offer page and order form.** After the three videos, release the fourth (the offer) and email everyone when it's live. Keep the page very simple: a video of you making the offer ("An Irresistible Offer...") and a link to the order form, which can sit under the video or on its own page. Add bumps and one-click upsells. Use a product launch to open a new business or product; after it has run live once, build an automated version for evergreen traffic.
