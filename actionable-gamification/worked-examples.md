# Worked examples: Chou's own audits and design

Use these as models for the shape and depth of a finished audit or design.

## Level I audit: Facebook

- **Strong:**
  - **CD5** at the center, through Social Prods, Social Treasures, and Conformity Anchors.
  - **CD7**: Nir Eyal's "Rewards of the Hunt," where pulling down to refresh the feed works like a slot-machine lever.
  - **CD4**: photos, and a profile that carries the user's social identity.
  - **CD3**: crafting witty posts for Likes.
  - **CD2**: popular posts.
  - **CD8** in the Endgame: the Sunk Cost Prison of friends, photos, and messages.
- **Weak: CD1 and CD6.** Early Facebook was Harvard-only, which put CD6 high in Discovery and built CD1 Elitism through Onboarding and Scaffolding. Lacking CD6 now is acceptable, because CD6 is not ideal for long-term engagement and Facebook is engaging existing users rather than rapidly capturing new ones.
- **Improvement (CD1):** critics treat Facebook as a waste of time (boycotts, "Facebook fasts"). One fix is a top bar showing a different community in dire need each day, with a trigger button to donate time or money, so Facebook can say it doesn't only change the world, it *saves* the world as users log in. Another is finding mentors or homework buddies, which makes Facebook feel "productive."

## Speed Camera Lottery (Volkswagen Fun Theory, Stockholm, 2010)

- **Problem:** get drivers under the speed limit. Define the problem and how success is measured first.
- **Existing drives:**
  - CD1: safe driving serves a cause beyond yourself.
  - CD8: fines are expensive to enforce, and the odds of being caught are low.
  - CD3: speed display signs give feedback, but the Win-State is unclear ("What was the speed limit again?"), and some drivers try to set a high score.
- **The four added drives:**
  - **CD4**: obeying drivers enter a lottery funded by speeders' fines.
  - **CD7**: a 10% chance of $100 beats a sure $10.
  - **CD2**: a green thumbs-up or red thumbs-down, with the speed shown in green or red, gives a clear Win-State.
  - **CD5**: on a busy street, no one wants to be seen as a "thumbs-down loser."
- **Result:** average speed fell over 20% in the pilot. It lacked Endgame design.
- **Lesson:** "constantly think about motivation and the 8 Core Drives instead of features, functions, and systems."

## Level II audit: Waze, phase by phase

Typical navigation apps run only on CD2 (progress, arriving efficiently) and CD8 (not getting lost). A new entrant needs more drives.

- **Discovery:**
  - mild CD7 ("A user-generated GPS? How does that work?", cute graphics)
  - CD5: reviews, ratings, and friends telling friends (Conformity Anchor)
- **Onboarding:**
  - CD1: the *Traffic* monster with "Beat Traffic, Together"
  - CD5: "363 Wazers nearby"
  - CD7 and CD6: other drivers' crowns and helmets ("Hmm, I wonder how I can make my car avatar to look different.")
  - CD2: big round buttons so the user feels smart
  - CD4: Status Points. The unlockable avatars are Dangling but gimmicky at this stage.
- **Scaffolding:**
  - CD2: side roads that beat the traffic jam
  - CD4: the Alfred Effect, with the 8:30 AM "go to work?" prompt and the Wednesday gym prompt
  - CD7: the Oracle Effect ("Will it be there?")
  - CD3: reporting police and hazards, which gives a sense of agency
  - CD5: thank-you pop-ups seconds after a report
  - CD6: "Waze Royalty" is weak, because the reward grants no power and no real status
  - CD8 turned into CD2: cheaper gas and short detours with the extra time shown
- **Endgame:** the drives are mostly CD1 (community pride), CD2 (as long as routes really are faster), CD3 (the map editor, which is limited), and CD4. CD8 comes from losing the system's intelligence and saved favorites. CD5 stays light (Beep-Beeps), and CD6 and CD7 fade. Percentile-based avatar ranks can make people drive more just to keep their rank, an unintended environmental cost. A White Hat-heavy Endgame is fine.
- **Improvements** (from a ten-minute brainstorm):
  - **CD1:** carry "helping a community beat traffic" through Scaffolding and the Endgame, not only Onboarding.
  - **CD2:** make arrival a bigger Win-State, with a victorious tune and "You have arrived safely. Awesome!" A 25% chance of "You're awesome" adds a CD7 Easter Egg.
  - **CD4:** give the app an "Intelligent Score" that rises as it learns the user, so the Alfred Effect is visible early. Add personal charts ("you went to the gym 105 times this year").
  - **CD3:** "Accelerwaze," where drivers challenge Waze's ETA with their own route. The risk is reckless driving, so weigh the unintended consequences.
  - **CD5:** carpool matching by interest, industry, or company, which also offsets the extra driving.
  - **CD7:** a surprise flash mob that reroutes Wazers to a beach ("Figure out why we brought all of you here") and awards an exclusive "Health Buff" avatar.
  - **CD6:** gate carpools and flash mobs behind higher levels. This gives leveling meaning and keeps meetups safe, because only proven contributors qualify.
  - **CD8:** it arrives on its own once the experience is joyful. Add no Black Hat to the Endgame; focus on White Hat and Right Brain.

## Designing from scratch: YukaiChou.com and Octalysis Prime

**Strategy Dashboard**
- **Business Metrics, by importance:**
  1. Octalysis Prime sign-ups
  2. book purchases
  3. workshop orders
  4. consulting leads
  5. email subscriptions
  6. social shares
  7. video views
  8. Octalysis page views
  9. overall site views

  The metrics at the bottom are enablers of the ones on top.
- **Users:**
  - company employees applying gamification
  - educators, nonprofits, and governments seeking social impact
  - individuals passionate about games and self-improvement
- **Desired Actions by phase:**
  - *Discovery:* hear about the work at a conference, on social media, or by referral; land on a content page; click the Octalysis link.
  - *Onboarding:* skim the article, share it, watch the TEDx talk, visit the About page, subscribe by email.
  - *Scaffolding:* open the weekly emails, read and share articles, join the Facebook group, buy the book, watch the free videos, take a workshop, earn the Level 1 Certificate.
  - *Endgame:* join Octalysis Prime, watch its videos, attend weekly office hours, apply a new Game Technique each week, join exclusive groups, study case studies, become a licensee, work with Yu-kai.
- **Feedback Mechanics:**
  - First, the mediums: the two websites, the book, the email list, Twitter and the Facebook group, talks, videos, workshops.
  - Then the mechanics, tagged with Core Drives: Countdown Timers (6, 8), Unlockable Content (3, 6), Virtual Dice Rolls (4, 7), Glowing Choice overlay (2, 7), Hello Bar (1, 2, 6, 7), Game Technique Collection Sets (2, 4, 6, 7), Leaderboards (2, 5, 6, 8), Certificates (2, 4, 5, 6), Character Growth Charts (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8), Exchangeable Points (2-8).
- **Rewards, from abundant to scarce:** Status Points, Achievement Symbols, community, being featured in emails, on the site, or in videos, Easter Egg surprises, a free eBook, a physical book, a signed book, a workshop discount, personal time with Yu-kai, a chance to work with him, an Octalysis License.

**Ideation pass**
- **CD1:**
  - Hello Bar messages about transforming your life, kept believable
  - showcases of pro bono work for causes
  - Elitism through "Good Gamification"
  - a new "Save the World Weekly" offering, added back into the Dashboard
- **CD2:**
  - the Desired Action never unclear, using Glowing Choice and Desert Oasis
  - a Win-State on every log-in
  - Knowledge Scores that lead to certificates
  - a profile shaped like an empty octagon that grows toward the drives a member studies
- **CD3:**
  - an Octalysis Tool for members' own projects, which adds a new Scaffolding Desired Action
  - feedback on members' work during office hours
  - members voting on what gets analyzed next ("turning passive observers into active players")
  - members applying the weekly technique and reporting back
  - specialists hosting sessions
  - monthly design challenges for nonprofits
- **CD4:**
  - Collection Sets of Game Techniques
  - certificates
  - Easter Egg free books, drawn from the rewards list
  - showing how the knowledge pays off in salary, fundraising, and parenting
  - a Personal Octalysis Report
  - Exchangeable Points, provisionally "Chou Coins"
- **CD5:**
  - a free Water Cooler group and a premium Mastermind
  - a Group Quest where the next level opens once X users are certified
  - "Success Buddies"
  - a showcase of random top-10% members, deliberately not a competitive leaderboard
  - regional group scores as Conformity Anchors
  - hashtags
  - tipping Chou Coins as Social Treasures, where each tipped coin doubles, up to a limit
- **CD6:**
  - an admission question ("In five sentences or less, share what you plan to do...") that sets a barrier of commitment without being hard enough to cause procrastination
  - Dangling locked content with exact instructions for unlocking it
  - certificates kept genuinely difficult
  - a combination of Torture Break, Anchored Juxtaposition, and Evanescent Opportunity: new content is free for one to three days on a countdown, then members-only, then public after a one-year countdown
  - secret keywords hidden in the videos that earn 50% off
  - daily one-hour Group Quests
- **CD7:**
  - videos whose backgrounds keep changing
  - Easter Egg creatures that drop Game Techniques when defeated
  - Mystery Box dice rolls after large Desired Actions
- **CD8:**
  - a FOMO Punch about what members lose each day without the knowledge
  - a full refund guarantee to address the Anti Core Drive
  - deliberately no Sunk Cost Prison

**Second pass: the emergent design.** Each Desired Action is tagged with the Core Drives it teaches, and the member's octagon grows in those directions. Reaching Level 10 in a drive grants that drive's power:

- **CD1:** vote on which nonprofit receives 3% of revenue.
- **CD2:** a gold profile.
- **CD3:** host your own sessions.
- **CD4:** a 30% coin bonus on every Desired Action.
- **CD5:** moderation rights and the ability to give Social Treasures.
- **CD6:** content unlocks for fewer coins.
- **CD7:** two lucky dice rolls a day instead of one.
- **CD8:** 40% off the membership fee.

Socializers and Achievers each play their own way. Mastering CD4, CD6, and CD7 together makes a Chain Combo: more rolls, larger wins, and cheaper unlocks.
